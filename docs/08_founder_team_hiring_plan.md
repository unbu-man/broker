# Chapter VIII — Solo Founder to Regulated Brokerage Team

**Founder question (18 November 2026):** Should the founder start alone, or recruit a team immediately?

**Decision:** Start the technical research and paper-trading prototype alone. Build a small network of professional advisers early; recruit or contract specialists only as evidence, risk, funding, and legal obligations justify them. Never operate a live crypto/forex brokerage as an unqualified solo operation.

## Purpose

BrokerAI is a planned global crypto and forex technology/brokerage business. The first-year target is a working **simulation-only** MVP and a credible licensing/partner plan, *not* an unsupervised real-money launch. Crypto spot, crypto derivatives, FX dealing, and CFDs may be subject to different local requirements.

## Founder-led phase: Months 1–3

Founder owns:
- Company vision, customer interviews and market/product selection.
- Scope control: crypto and forex only; no stock trading at MVP.
- Data engineering: sourced historical BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, EUR/USD and USD/JPY feeds.
- Core architecture, paper orders, virtual ledger, risk-engine test suite.
- Honest backtesting and signals research, GitHub documentation, milestones and demos.
- Cost tracking and outreach to prospective technology partners.

**Do not do alone:** give legal opinions, design a real-money compliance program without specialists, handle customer assets, or present untested AI signals as guaranteed income.

### First 90 days: verifiable exits

1. A documented customer persona and five or more customer discovery conversations.
2. A documented product and jurisdiction research shortlist with no live authorization assumed.
3. A runnable market-data ingestion prototype with reproducible tests.
4. A deterministic demo order and virtual ledger prototype with reconciliation tests.
5. A public/non-sensitive GitHub README, architecture, backlog, and decisions log.
6. A basic budget that protects personal living expenses and excludes speculative licensing capital.
7. An initial conversation with qualified counsel/regulated-market experts before committing to licensing or client solicitation.

## Hiring priority (when the trigger appears)

| Priority | Role | When to bring them in | Suggested arrangement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate access, not necessarily payroll | Qualified fintech/crypto/FX legal & regulatory adviser | Before market-entry decisions, marketing regulated services, taking deposits, signing broker contracts | Paid consultations scoped to jurisdiction/product |
| 1 | Senior backend/trading systems engineer | Simulator and accounting design exceed solo capacity, or code is nearing beta | Part-time or contract, possible co-founder only after collaboration |
| 2 | Security/DevSecOps specialist | Any internet-facing login, secrets, API keys, or beta accounts | Project-based review and hardening |
| 3 | Quantitative research/risk specialist | Automated strategy pilots, complex exposure, or leveraged-product design | Adviser / contract / senior employee as needed |
| 4 | Product/UI engineer and support operations | User interviews show sustained demand, user onboarding is a bottleneck | Contract then hire |
| Mandatory before live launch, depending on jurisdiction | Licensed responsible managers, compliance/AML officers, finance/controller, risk, independent security and customer-asset operations | When a relevant regulator and counsel specify approval/staffing needs | Appropriately qualified personnel; roles and separation of duties as required |

The exact job titles, independence requirements, capital, local presence and residency requirements depend on the licensing jurisdiction and business activities.

## Hiring vs co-founder vs adviser

**Employee or contractor:** time-bound deliverables, documented ownership/IP assignment, confidentiality, security policies and compensation.

**Adviser:** narrow areas like licensing, legal review or counterparty relationships. Verify credentials and conflicts.

**Co-founder:** only if someone commits long-term, consistently delivers, and brings a critical capability and shared company values. Discuss equity vesting, decision rights, founder departures, IP ownership and dispute resolution *with counsel* before granting equity. Do not hand out large shares solely for an introduction or early enthusiasm.

## Stage gates for team growth

- **Solo → collaborator:** at least a basic architecture, testable demonstration and clear backlog exist.
- **Collaborator → core founding team:** validated problem, collaboration history, realistic budget and dedicated responsibilities.
- **Core team → regulated operations team:** written counsel/authority guidance, funding, partners and compliance design are sufficiently mature.
- **Live launch:** product/jurisdiction/entity authorization, required qualified staff, audited systems, transaction monitoring, consumer safeguards and financial capacity are verified. No date-based shortcut.

## Suggested founder weekly schedule (adapt to available hours)

- 2 sessions: Data pipelines, trading core and tests.
- 1 session: Quant research, market microstructure and backtesting discipline.
- 1 session: Customer discovery and competitive/business analysis.
- 1 session: Regulatory reading, evidence register, adviser questions and budget.
- 1 session: Documentation, code review, retrospective and next sprint.

## Risk management for the founder

- Do not commit emergency savings, tuition or living expenses to speculative brokerage capital.
- Keep demonstration-only systems clearly labeled, no real funds or brokerage service claims.
- Use small, reversible contracting commitments rather than premature permanent hiring.
- Do not represent a global product as legally available in every country.
- Protect trade secrets and security details; no client PII or API secrets in public GitHub.
