# Chapter II — Global Regulation and Market-Entry Strategy

**Research framework, not legal advice. Verify up-to-date requirements directly with counsel and relevant regulators before making offers or accepting clients.**

## Key principle
A license in one jurisdiction does **not** generally authorize retail brokerage services everywhere else. Rules may depend on where the legal entity is established, where the customer is resident, product, solicitation method and functions performed.

## Country × product × entity permission engine
Each onboarding or commercial feature must pass a rule check:
- Legal entity providing service
- Customer residence and any citizenship/sanctions factors required by law
- Allowed products and customer category
- Licensed partner or own permission and scope
- Marketing / solicitation restrictions
- KYC/AML screening, suitability/appropriateness where required
- Custody/segregation, complaints, transaction reporting and tax obligations
- Local data privacy and consumer protection.

If an authorization is unknown, **deny live activity by default**.

## Legal and professional diligence
- Request written legal opinions for selected launch and client jurisdictions.
- Determine minimum paid-up capital, regulatory capital, security bonds, audit, governance, staffed key roles and local presence.
- Confirm whether external liquidity, central counterparty, execution venue or clearing agreements are required.
- Clarify if B-Book or own-account internalization is lawful; document conflicts and hedge/risk policy.
- Separately classify signal services, financial promotions, robo-advice, copy trading, discretionary management and payments.
- Verify sanctions exposure and AML/CTF/KYC processes.
- Plan incident reporting, cybersecurity audits, disaster recovery, segregation/reconciliation and withdrawal processing.
- Decide whether a technology-vendor or introducing-arrangement route is legally permitted before signing agreements.

## Release gate
**No live client account, deposit, marketing of regulated activity, personalized trade recommendations, or real-money routing** unless a documented legal-authorization matrix explicitly permits the entity, customer and product, with operational controls verified.
